# Generative AI Use Case Studies

## 1. Site Introduction  
Generative AI Use Case Studies is a curated exploration of how modern AI is reshaping education and research. We cover five pillars—what LLMs can do today, where they fall short, how to engineer prompts that unlock their full power, hands-on case studies from CUHK and peers, and a forward look at next-gen models, retrieval-first systems like DeepSeek, and the broader AI ecosystem. Whether you’re a teacher, researcher or developer, you’ll find concrete examples, practical guides and real-world data to inform your AI journey.
