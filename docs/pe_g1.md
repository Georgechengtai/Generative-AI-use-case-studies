???+ tip "For Knowing What You Demand"

    When you want thoughtful, targeted answers, encourage the AI to ask questions first:

    ```yaml
    [YOUR MAIN REQUEST HERE]
    
    Before proceeding with this task, please ask me any questions you need to provide the most helpful response possible. Consider aspects like context, specific requirements, format preferences, and any constraints I should be aware of.
    ```

    This mimics how human experts work – they rarely jump straight to solutions without understanding your specific situation first. The questions the AI asks often reveal aspects of your request you hadn't considered.

???+ info "For Learning Complex Topics"
    ```yaml
    Once we've clarified the details, please present your response in a step-by-step format, pausing after each step so I can process the information before moving to the next point.
    ```

    This turns overwhelming information into a manageable learning path, similar to how a good teacher checks for understanding before moving forward.
