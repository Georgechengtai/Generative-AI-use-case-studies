# How to Fine-tune Your Prompt? 

!!! info "Original Source"
    From The Neuron Newspaper.

!!! quote "Editor's Note"

    The purpose of this prompt is to make very small, precise adjustments to your prompt to optimise its performance and consistency. The idea is simple: tell what you expect but what you got. AI will fine-tune your prompt as requested. However, depends on the circumstance, it may push too hard. You still have to experiment with it to get what you want.
    
    

???+ tip "Fine-tune a prompt by presenting your desired output and actual output."

    ```yaml
    “Here's a prompt: [YOUR PROMPT]. The desired behavior is [X], but instead it [Y]. What minimal edits would you make to encourage the agent to more consistently address these shortcomings?”
    ```
