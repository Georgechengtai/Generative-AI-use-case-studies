# DeepSeek: Privacy & Safety Considerations

## Understanding DeepSeek's Data Practices

If you're planning to use DeepSeek's official services, it's important to understand how they handle your data.

!!! warning "Data Collection Policy"
    According to [DeepSeek's privacy policy](https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html), the company collects:
    
    - Basic device information (operating system, device model)
    - Network information including IP addresses
    - Content that users input, including prompts and uploaded files
    - User location based on IP address
    
    This data is stored on servers located in China.

## Using DeepSeek: Options and Considerations

### Standard Cloud-Based Usage

Most users access DeepSeek through its website or mobile app. This approach offers:

- Immediate access without technical setup
- Full access to the most powerful model variants
- Regular updates and improvements

The trade-off is that your queries and uploaded content are processed on DeepSeek's servers, subject to their privacy policy.

### Local Deployment

!!! info "Alternative Approaches"
    For users with stringent privacy requirements, DeepSeek's open-source nature enables local deployment on your own hardware. This advanced option keeps your data entirely within your control, though it requires technical expertise to implement.

    This approach is safer than using ChatGPT (non-enterprise plan) or POE, and it enables you to feed sensitive documents such as exam results, payment vouchers, etc. because all information is processed in your local environment and never leaked out. But once again, we must remind you that you must possess a level of techincal expertise to prevent misuse of the tools.

## Best Practices for Privacy

Whether you're a teacher, researcher, or student, these privacy practices can help you use DeepSeek responsibly:

???+ tip "General Privacy Tips"
    - Review DeepSeek's privacy policy before use
    - Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in prompts
    - Consider what you're comfortable sharing before uploading documents
    - Be mindful of intellectual property when sharing creative work

???- example "For Research Projects"
    - Discuss privacy implications with your supervisor
    - Consider anonymising data before analysis
    - Document your data practices for transparency
    - Check institutional guidelines regarding third-party tools

???- warning "For Classroom Activities"
    - Inform students about data handling practices
    - Provide alternatives for students with privacy concerns
    - Create clear guidelines about appropriate usage
    - Focus on activities that don't require personal information

???- note "Finding the Right Balance"
    - Consider the nature of your activities and the sensitivity of your data
    - Weigh convenience against privacy concerns for your specific context
    - Start with low-stakes activities while learning about the tool

By understanding these considerations, university teachers and students can make informed decisions about incorporating DeepSeek into their work while protecting sensitive information.
