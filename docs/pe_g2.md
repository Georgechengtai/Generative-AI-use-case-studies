!!! quote "Editor's Note"
    These techniques help you access more specialised knowledge by creating AI personas with particular perspectives and expertise.

### Finding the Right Expert

Think of AI knowledge as existing in a vast multi-dimensional space. Generic prompts access general knowledge, but by creating specialised personas, you can "navigate" to specific regions of this knowledge space where more nuanced understanding exists.

???+ example "Expert Discovery"
    ```yaml
    Who would be the most suitable expert or professional to help me accomplish [YOUR TASK]?
    ```

    This prompt helps identify which "knowledge region" would be most helpful for your task. For example, a general question about website performance might benefit from an expert in web development, UX design, or SEO, depending on your actual goals.

### Summoning Your Specialist

Once you've identified the right type of expert, you can create a more vivid and capable persona:

???+ example "The Expert Summoning Technique"
    ```yaml
    1. Give me 20 words describing a [specific specialist].
    
    2. Create a 4-sentence prompt using those words to summon this specialist.
    
    3. [Copy the resulting prompt into a new chat]
    ```

    This method works like creating a focused "knowledge lens" through which the AI views your requests. Rather than getting generic information about taxes, you can interact with a virtual tax accountant who approaches your questions with the specific perspective and knowledge of that profession.

### Applying Your Expert to Specific Tasks

With your expert identified and summoned, you can set up a structured interaction:

???+ tip "Role-Based Framework"
    ```yaml
    You are now [EXPERT], with expertise in [CLEARLY DESCRIBE CONTEXT/BACKGROUND]. Your role is to help me complete [YOUR TASK]. Each time I provide you with [CLEARLY DEFINE YOUR INPUT], you respond using [CLEARLY DEFINE EXPECTED OUTPUT STRUCTURE].
    ```

    This framework keeps the AI focused within the specific "knowledge region" you've targeted, providing consistent expertise throughout your conversation.
