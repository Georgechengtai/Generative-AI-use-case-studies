# How to Master Any Topics via Reasoning Models

!!! abstract "Original Source & Purpose"
    These prompts are from [Robin Delta's post on X](https://x.com/heyrobinai/status/1886354934846914700) using OpenAI o3-mini, one of the frontier reasoning models. He offers a set of effective prompts designed to help users master any topic through structured learning approaches with reasoning models.

???+ tip "Editor's pick"
    My personal favorite are No. 4 & 5 (preferably via NotebookLM). To learn a concept via socrative method and then examine my understanding immediately helps me prevent confirmation bias deeply rooted in my brain.

---

???+ success "Learning Prompt Collection"
    
    ???+ tip "1. Roleplay as a Mentor"
        Assume the role of an experienced mentor in [specific field]. Share your wisdom by offering practical advice, actionable strategies, and insightful tips for newcomers. Describe common challenges and pitfalls in the field, and provide clear guidance on how to overcome them, drawing on real-world examples and best practices.
    
    ???+ info "2. Master any topic"
        Act as an expert in [subject]. Briefly explain the key concepts, including a real-world example for each. Then, provide a concise, step-by-step 30-day plan to master the topic, outlining daily or weekly milestones, suggested resources, and practical exercises.
    
    ???+ example "3. Challenge and Improve - Critical Thinking"
        Challenge me with a series of thought-provoking questions on [subject]. After each question, offer feedback on my answer--highlighting both strengths and areas for improvement--and suggest actionable ways to enhance my critical thinking on this topic.
    
    ???+ warning "4. Socratic Enlightenment - Uncover and Understand"
        Guide me through a Socratic dialogue on [concept]. Ask insightful, probing questions that help me explore the deeper layers of this idea and gradually refine my understanding step-by-step.
    
    ???+ note "5. Teach-back Mastery - Validate and Clarify"
        I will attempt to teach you the concept of [topic]. Please correct any inaccuracies in my explanation and ask clarifying questions that deepen my understanding of the material.
    
    ???+ abstract "6. Connect Past and Present (Historical Event)"
        Provide an in-depth analysis of how [historical event] impacted [field of study]. Draw insightful parallels to modern-day applications and discuss key lessons we can learn from this event.
    
    ???+ quote "7. Personalised Learning Plan"
        Help me design a personalised learning plan for mastering [subject]. Break it down into daily tasks, recommend valuable resources, and suggest practical exercises to steadily build my skills.
