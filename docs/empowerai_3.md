# Bachelor thesis writing (Political Science)

!!! quote "Editor's Note"

    You may think this is a bit lacking. I think so, but the source does not offer how teachers oversighted (or they did not).

???+ info "What was done"
    **Author:** Josefina Sipinen  
    **School:** University of Tampere  
    **Tools used:** Large language models (general)  
    **Events:** In February 2025, a pilot workshop was run for bachelor’s **political science** students preparing to write their thesis. A typical thesis at Tampere is 20–30 pages. The workshop lasted two hours and combined short lectures with practical exercises.

???+ tip "Implementation"
    The workshop began with an introduction to AI and its possible role in thesis writing. Students then tried out AI tools to help them develop research questions, design frameworks, and plan their analysis.  

    After the hands‑on practice, students discussed their results in small groups. They shared what worked, what failed, and how they might use AI responsibly in their own projects. The session ended with a collective reflection on the benefits and risks of using AI in thesis work.

???+ note "Key take‑aways"
    - AI helps students break a large thesis project into smaller, more manageable steps.  
    - Oversight and clear guidance are needed so students don’t misuse AI or rely on it too much.  
    - AI literacy — knowing how and when to use AI — reduces uncertainty and supports independent learning.
