# DeepSeek's Market Impact

!!! info "Why This Matters"
    DeepSeek demonstrated that frontier AI models could be trained at a fraction of the expected cost using less powerful hardware, challenging assumptions about computational requirements for advanced AI development.

## NVIDIA's Historical Stock Crash

The release of DeepSeek-R1 triggered one of the most dramatic market events in recent history:

- NVIDIA's stock plummeted **17%** in a single day (January 27, 2025)
- This resulted in a staggering **~$600 billion market cap loss**
- Marked the **[single-biggest day drop in U.S. market history]**(https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/27/nvidia-sheds-almost-600-billion-in-market-cap-biggest-drop-ever.html)


## Mobile App Success

DeepSeek's mobile application quickly dominated app stores worldwide:

- Displaced ChatGPT as the #1 free app in Apple's App Store on Jun 26
- As of February 5, 2025, the DeepSeek AI app has been downloaded over 21.66 million times worldwide across Google Play and the App Store.

This [extraordinary growth](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/27/deepseek-displaces-chatgpt-as-the-app-stores-top-app/), demonstrated the public's immediate interest in DeepSeek's capabilities and accessibility. New entrants can **quickly capture market share** from established players and industry leaders like ChatGPT and Gemini are **not immune to disruption**.
