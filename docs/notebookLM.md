## What is NotebookLM?

NotebookLM organizes and processes documents to make studying more effective. It:

- **Organises Information:** Creates separate notebooks for different projects
- **Processes Various Sources:** Handles documents, web links and videos
- **Answers Questions:** Provides insights based directly on your materials
- **Generates (Audio) Summaries:** Creates concise overviews of complex content

*In May 2025, Google declare that it is developing video overviews features and enables public notebook discoveries!* (Source: https://www.testingcatalog.com/google-is-developing-video-overviews-feature-for-notebooklm/) 

Students, researchers and professionals use it to manage information and extract key insights quickly.

## Recommended Features

NotebookLM offers several practical features that enhance self-study:

??? info "Multi-format Sources"
    Upload Google Docs, Google Slides, PDF and web links to your notebooks. The system processes various media types to give you more comprehensive insights about your topics.

??? info "Mind Map Analysis"
    Visualise connections between ideas with the mind map feature. This helps you understand relationships between concepts across your documents.

??? info "Format Flexibility"
    Request information in different formats like summaries, bullet points, study guides or FAQs. This lets you transform complex information into more digestible formats.

??? info "Audio Conversion"
    Turn your notes into audio in over 50 languages. This feature helps you study while commuting or doing other activities when reading isn't convenient.

## Why It Helps Studying

NotebookLM transforms how you study by:

??? info "Reducing Information Overload"
    NotebookLM organises documents into separate notebooks with up to 50 sources each. This structure helps you focus only on relevant materials for each project without getting overwhelmed by unrelated content.

??? info "Saving Time"
    The platform quickly generates summaries and highlights key points from lengthy documents. Instead of reading entire papers, you can extract the most important information in seconds and decide where to focus your deeper reading.

??? info "Supporting Active Learning"
    NotebookLM's Q&A feature transforms passive reading into interactive study sessions. Ask specific questions about your materials to explore concepts more deeply, test your understanding, and engage with content in a way that improves retention.

??? info "Improving Comprehension"
    Access information in multiple formats (text summaries, bullet points, audio, mind maps) to accommodate different learning preferences. This variety helps you process complex information through multiple channels, reinforcing your understanding.

NotebookLM works best as a complement to traditional studying. Use it to organise your materials, extract key points, and create study aids that help you understand complex topics more efficiently.
