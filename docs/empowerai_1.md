# Critical thinking (broad-based)

!!! quote "Editor's Note"

    To let students take advantage of AI, they could not oursource their thinking process entirely. That's why teachers must let they think, compare and evaluate on top of AI output. Reverse thinking in an altered sense - to think of how to construct a prompt that will give desired, qualified output - are benefitical for critical thinking too as it "improves" the way of thinking of students.

???+ info "What was done"
    **Author:** Outi Veivo, Jere Riekkinen & Leena Salmi  
    **School:** University of Turku  
    **Tools used:** Large language models

    **Events:** A survey of 27 teachers and PhD students across Humanities, **Social Sciences**, the AI Academy, and the AInspiration community. The survey explored how AI can help develop students’ critical thinking skills. The activities were grouped into five areas: argumentation & reasoning, analysis & synthesis, metacognition, source evaluation, and AI literacy.

???+ tip "Implementation"

    Teachers designed activities that required students to actively engage with AI, not just accept its outputs.  

    - In debates, AI acted as a “devil’s advocate,” forcing students to defend their reasoning.  
    - Students compared AI definitions to textbook explanations, expanded AI outlines, and contrasted abstracts written by AI with those written by humans.  
    - Students documented their prompts, reflected on how their prior knowledge shaped AI use, and compared AI’s reasoning with their own.  
    - They evaluated AI’s claims by checking references, detecting fabricated citations, and spotting hidden biases.  
    - Students also discussed ethical issues, integrity, and the commercial motives behind AI tools.  

???+ note "Key take‑aways"
    - AI can either support or undermine critical thinking, depending on task design.  
    - Students learn more when they are asked to critique, reflect, and compare AI outputs.  
    - AI literacy — questioning and evaluating AI-generated content — is now a core academic skill.
