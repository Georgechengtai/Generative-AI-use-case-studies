### A Visual Approach to Understanding Economics

Dr. Mok suggests that instead of relying solely on text or equations, instructors can present **AI‑generated debate illustrations**.  
These images spark curiosity, make theories memorable, and lower barriers to participation.

#### Example Visuals

Below are three sample prompts and outputs that illustrate how economic debates can be visualised.

???+ example "Case 1: Ricardo vs. List"
    ```text
    A scholarly illustration of David Ricardo debating Friedrich List.

    Ricardo’s side: a comparative advantage table showing opportunity costs.
    List’s side: the phrase “Infant Industry Protection” with a tariff diagram (supply/demand with tariff wedge).
    Banner: “Free Trade vs. Protectionism”.
    Style: academic, clean diagrams, minimal text.
    ```
    ![Free Trade vs. Protectionism](images/ricardo_list.jpg)

???+ example "Case 2: Samuelson vs. Lucas"
    ```text
    A modern infographic illustration of Paul Samuelson debating Robert Lucas.

    Samuelson’s side: the Keynesian multiplier formula k = 1 / (1 − MPC) with an IS‑LM diagram.
    Lucas’s side: the phrase “Rational Expectations” with the New Classical Phillips Curve equation *πt = πt^e − β(u − u)**.
    Banner: “Stabilization Policy vs. Policy Ineffectiveness”.
    Style: clean, academic, textbook‑like.
    ```
    ![Stablization Policy vs Policy Ineffectiveness](images/samuelson_lucas.jpg)

???+ example "Case 3: Keynes vs. Hayek"
    ```text
    A digital illustration of John Maynard Keynes and Friedrich Hayek at podiums during a debate. Each has a speech bubble: Keynes’s bubble says “Government Intervention!” and Hayek’s says “Free Markets!”. Above them, a banner reads “The Great Economic Debate”. ### Learning Objectives

???+ tip "Key Outcomes"
    - Develop **prompting skills** for visual clarity  
    - Strengthen recall of major economic debates  
    - Encourage critical comparison of opposing theories  
    - Use humor and visuals to make class discussion more engaging  


### Implementation Notes

???+ warning "Considerations"
    - AI outputs may require editing for accuracy  
    - Make clear that images are **aids, not authoritative sources**  
    - Humor should be balanced with academic seriousness  
```
