# AI Trends: The "Vibe" Shift (Late 2025)

???- quote "Featuring"

    *   **Gemini 3 Pro** (Google) - The rise of "Vibe Coding".
    *   **MeituAI** (Meitu) - The "3-Grid Snow" viral trend.

!!! note "Editor's Note"

    Late 2025 brings a shift in how we interact with AI. We are seeing tools move away from technical complexity towards intuitive, intent-based control. Whether in software development or social media, the focus is shifting from *how* to build (syntax/editing) to *what* to build (intent/curation).

## Gemini 3 Pro & "Vibe Coding"

The release of **Gemini 3 Pro** has solidified a new way of building software known as "Vibe Coding".

### What is it?
Traditionally, coding required you to be a "bricklayer"—writing every line of logic and syntax yourself. With tools like **Google Antigravity**, you become the "architect". You describe the functionality and the "vibe" of the application, and the AI handles the implementation.

??? info "Tool: Google Antigravity"

    **[Google Antigravity](https://deepmind.google/technologies/antigravity/)** is an "Agentic IDE" (Integrated Development Environment). 
    
    Think of it as a smarter version of **VS Code** (the software most people use to write code). In VS Code, you type the code yourself. In Antigravity, you manage a team of AI agents who write the code for you.

### The Shift in Skills
This does not mean coding is "dead". It means the difficulty has shifted. You no longer hunt for missing semicolons (syntax errors); you hunt for misunderstood instructions (logic errors).

!!! info "Managerial Debugging"

    This requires a new skill: looking at a broken app and figuring out which part of your *instruction* confused the AI, rather than which line of code is wrong.

## MeituAI & The "Snow Grid" Trend

On the consumer side, this same shift is visible in viral trends like the **"3-Grid Snow" (雪景三格圖)** feature from **MeituAI**.

### What is it?
This feature, popular on Instagram and TikTok, automatically transforms a user's photo into a cinematic, snow-filled triptych.

??? example "Visualising the Trend"

    Imagine a standard portrait photo. The AI:

    1.  **Segments** the subject perfectly from the background.
    2.  **Generates** a realistic, consistent winter scene behind them.
    3.  **Composes** the image into a three-panel vertical strip (a classic cinematic layout).
    4.  **Animates** falling snow over the static image (when combined with video tools).

### How it works
This demonstrates **Accessible Multimodality**. Complex tasks that used to require Photoshop (masking), Blender (particle effects), and Premiere Pro (layout) are now compressed into a single "filter".

??? tip "The 'Power User' Workflow"

    Creators are combining tools to act as "AI Directors":

    1.  **Composition:** Use **[Meitu](https://www.meitu.com/en)** to generate the static 3-grid layout.
    2.  **Animation:** Upload the image to **[Google Veo](https://deepmind.google/technologies/veo/)** or **[Runway Gen-3](https://runwayml.com/)** with the prompt "gentle falling snow".
    3.  **Result:** A high-production-value video created in minutes on a phone.

## Editor's Take

The convergence is clear. Whether you are using **Gemini 3 Pro** to build a web app or **MeituAI** to build a brand, the core competency is **Curation**. The AI provides the raw labour; you provide the taste and the direction.

!!! quote "Observation"

    We are entering an era where "technical skills" are being replaced by "directorial skills". The best creators in 2026 will not be the ones who can type the fastest, but the ones with the clearest vision of what they want to build.
