## What is DeepSeek?

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI company that emerged in early 2025 with an advanced reasoning model called DeepSeek R1. The model quickly gained attention for achieving capabilities comparable to leading commercial models while requiring significantly less computational resources.

DeepSeek has made waves in the AI community through its [remarkable market impact](deepseek-impact.md), causing significant shifts in technology stock valuations and quickly becoming the most-downloaded AI app in multiple countries. What makes DeepSeek particularly noteworthy is how it has [democratised advanced AI capabilities](deepseek-implications.md) by providing powerful models at a fraction of the cost of competitors.

For hobbyists, DeepSeek presents both opportunities and considerations. While it offers delightful output and performance for [various DIY applications and integation to existing services](deepseek-implications.md), users should be aware of important [privacy issues](deepseek-privacy.md).
