## Get Better Prompts Without Writing Them Yourself

If you've spent any time working with AI tools, you've likely encountered two major frustrations:

1. **The blank canvas problem**: Staring at an empty prompt box and trying to formulate your request effectively
2. **The retrieval problem**: Knowing you've created an excellent prompt before but unable to find it again

These issues waste time and diminish the value of AI assistance. Here are four practical solutions:

### 1. Let AI Improve Your Prompts (Almost Automatically)

Why start from scratch when you can have tools that automatically enhance your simple instructions into expertly crafted prompts? These prompt perfection tools act like "Grammarly for prompts," analysing what you type and suggesting improvements.

???+ info "The Basics"
    **What they are:** Software tools that enhance your basic instructions into well-crafted prompts.
    
    **How they help:** They translate your natural language into structured prompts that get better results from AI models, saving you the trouble of learning prompt engineering best practices.
    
    **Where to find them:** Available as Chrome extensions (like [Prompt Genie](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/prompt-genie-grammarly-bu/inafdkdkghgibhijaplaobmomoahefin)), standalone applications (like [Maskara.ai](https://www.maskara.ai/)), or as features within AI platforms like OpenAI's ChatGPT ([Custom GPTs](https://gptstore.ai/gpts/) in *Pro Plan*)

??? tip "Custom GPTs are Great"

    Custom GPTs represent perhaps the most elegant solution to prompt management in a realistic workflow. They function as pre-configured AI assistants with built-in instructions, knowledge, and capabilities for specific tasks.
    
    Instead of remembering and typing the same context-setting instructions repeatedly, a Custom GPT has this context permanently built in. Think of them as apps in an AI app store—each one designed for a particular purpose.
    
    Unfortunately, we are discouraged to discuss on VPN usage, and Hong Kong is geo-blocked by OpenAI (and many US services too).

### 2. "Steal" from Community Prompt Libraries, Quick and Dirty

Why reinvent the wheel when others have already crafted effective prompts for common scenarios? Community prompt libraries offer collections of proven, effective prompts created by other users that you can adopt and adapt for your own use.

???+ info "The Basics"
    **What they are:** Websites and repositories containing ready-to-use prompts created and refined by other users.
    
    **How they help:** They provide tested, effective prompts for common tasks, eliminating the need to create everything from scratch.
    
    **Where to find them:** Reddit communities like [r/ChatGPTPromptGenius](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPTPromptGenius/), [online curated collection of effective prompts](https://prompts.chat/), and advanced tools like the Prompt-to-Leaderboard tool on LM Arena.

## Save and Reuse Your Best Prompts Efficiently

### 3. Inject Saved Prompts Wherever You Need with Text Expanders

Imagine typing a short abbreviation like `;summarise` and having it instantly expand into a perfectly crafted prompt with all the necessary parameters and instructions. That's the magic of text expanders.

??? quote "Editor's Experience"
    What I love about text expanders is their flexibility. I can create a long string of pre-designed text within seconds and design it with a [placeholder] that my cursor automatically jumps to. This means I can instantly populate the template with specific content.

    Do notice that there'll be a lot of hard-coding! Consider this to be a nerd option for those never wish to click on a graphical interface with a mouse.

???+ info "The Basics"
    **What they are:** Applications that replace short abbreviations with longer text snippets.
    
    **How they help:** They allow you to store complex prompts and insert them anywhere with just a few keystrokes.
    
    **Where to find them:** Espanso (open-source, cross-platform) is particularly well-suited, but many options exist including TextExpander, PhraseExpress, and more.

??? example "Setting Up Espanso"
    [This article](https://maxziebell.de/2022/12/21/setting-up-an-ai-expansion-using-espanso/) suggests two powerful approaches to chat with AI:
    
    1. **Simple text expansion:** Replace shortcuts with your favourite prompts
    2. **API integration:** Set up triggers that not only expand text but can send your prompt to AI APIs and insert the response directly
    
    The linked guide provides detailed instructions for both approaches, including code examples and configuration files to help you get started.

### 4. Make Your Own Prompt Database

For those who work extensively with AI and develop numerous specialised prompts, a more robust organisation system becomes essential. Notion databases offer a flexible, powerful solution for prompt management.

??? quote "Editor's Word"
    This approach is really for power users. I won't share too many details here because this method is for those who want a local, structured, easily retrievable management of ready-made prompts. It's particularly useful if you're testing and refining prompts or sharing them with colleagues, as Notion makes it simple to collaborate and publish online.
    
    If you're serious about gaining complete control over your AI interactions, I'd recommend watching the full video linked below.

???+ info "The Basics"
    **What they are:** Structured knowledge management systems for organising your prompts.
    
    **How they help:** They provide searchable, categorised storage for your growing collection of effective prompts.
    
    **Where to find them:** Notion is particularly popular for this purpose due to its flexible database features.
