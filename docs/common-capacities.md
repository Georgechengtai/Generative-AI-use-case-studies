# What LLMs Can Actually Do Today

!!! note "Editor's Note"

    Content creation tools that once required expertise just years ago now work with simple text prompts from anyone. Below showcase highlights capabilities available right now, not theoretical future applications. 
    
    We only include LLM capabilities on this page. For limitations, we've collected opinions from our Co-PIs in a [common FAQ page](common-faqs.md). That section also contains detailed explanations on AI's [explainability](explainability.md) and [hallucination](hallucination.md) issues.
    
    Our purpose is to let you know how LLMs can make things done, not to push you to immediately add them to your workflow or set them as student assignments. We're simply letting you know these tools exist. If particular functions interest you, click the embedded videos to learn more and decide whether they're worth trying.

???+ info "Potential Issues: Geo-blocking and Premium Plan"

    Please note that HK is getting geo-blocked on many US AI services (VPN services required) and not all of the applications mentioned below offer a free-tier subscription plan.
